Traffic projection

A real app only sends some fraction of its traffic to an AI model, and that fraction is usually a handful of distinct request shapes, not one. AIRI can't know how many of each you'll get — that's your own usage data or projections — but once you tell it, it'll do the multiplication. Add one row per distinct AI call-site in your app (a representative sample request + how many you expect), and get a total.

Load-test token usage report (demo)

Real flow: your team integrates AIRI's /analyze into your service calls, your test suite runs normal and peak load scenarios (any language, any tool — k6, JMeter, Locust, your own scripts), and each call's AIRI result gets tagged with a label and collected. At the end of the run, submit everything you collected to /report and get back one consolidated report — a downloadable, professional PDF included. No accounts, no server-side storage: you submit the records, AIRI does the arithmetic. This section simulates that whole flow so you can see the report it produces — pick a couple of services below and run it.
